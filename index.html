📖 What's the Story?

Finals week has arrived, and the submission deadline is closing in!

After pulling three straight all-nighters, you finally completed your stack of heavily annotated notebooks and last-minute requirements. Across the classroom, your professors are patrolling the aisles—and if they catch you face-to-face before your work is ready, they’ll hit you with a brutal surprise oral recitation that drains your energy!

Armed with your high-energy study notebooks 📓, you must weave through rows of wooden desks, duck behind concrete pillars for cover, and send out a wave of finished assignments from a safe distance to get every professor’s attention before the 2:30 bell rings.

Submit to every professor to earn an A+ and claim your spot on the Dean’s Lister Honor Roll—or lose all your lives and retake the semester!

How to Play

  • Move←↑↓→ or WASD. On phones/tablets, use the on-screen D-pad.
  • DropPress Space or tap Drop assignment. Your notebook 📓 ticks, then blasts in a cross shape.
  • ScoreDesk 🪑 +50, professor +200, clear every professor +1000.
  • WinGrade every professor before the clock runs out.
  • LoseYou have 3 lives. You lose one if a professor catches you, your own blast hits you, or time runs out.
  • TipPillars 🗿 stop blasts, so duck behind them. Professors speed up as fewer remain.
