How to Play

  • Move←↑↓→ or WASD. On phones/tablets, use the on-screen D-pad.
  • DropPress Space or tap Drop assignment. Your notebook 📓 ticks, then blasts in a cross shape.
  • ScoreDesk 🪑 +50, professor +200, clear every professor +1000.
  • WinGrade every professor before the clock runs out.
  • LoseYou have 3 lives. You lose one if a professor catches you, your own blast hits you, or time runs out.
  • TipPillars 🗿 stop blasts, so duck behind them. Professors speed up as fewer remain.
